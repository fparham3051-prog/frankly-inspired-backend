Strategy for organizations that want to last.

Frankly Inspired and Associates, LLC works with mission-driven institutions that need more than a short-term fix. Engagements are designed around the organization's context, capacity, leadership needs, and sustainability goals.

Service Pillars

Sustainability Strategy

Organizational assessment, strategic priorities, risk identification, sustainability roadmaps, and implementation planning.

Revenue & Growth

Individual donor stewardship and retention, fundraising strategy, grant and public-funding strategy, revenue-pipeline design, and the cross-sector partnerships (education, workforce, government, corporate, philanthropic, and community) that sustain them.

Leadership & Operations

Executive advising and change leadership, paired with the workflows, KPIs, CRM/Salesforce practices, and AI readiness guidance that keep an organization running well. 1:1 leadership coaching is available as its own engagement.
